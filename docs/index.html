Version 2026.09.28: retake review that finds real restarts, phrase reword, a new recorder, project rename and optional vim motions. See the release notes for details.

Keep your best take

Retake review finds real restarts, even long ones or ones after "sorry, let me redo that". Play each take, see what differs, keep one.

Record naturally

A live waveform and a zoned level meter show your gain before the take. Pause, resume, and keep every take until you choose.

Export your way

Export WAV, MP3, transcript text, EDL, or Final Cut XML. Your audio stays on your computer.

Reword without re-recording

Select a phrase and rewrite it. The audio and its timing stay exactly as spoken; the edit is marked and reversible.

Vim motions, if you want them

Turn them on in Settings: h j k l, sentence and paragraph motions, visual selection, d, c and r operators, counts, undo and redo.

Windows and macOS

Native installers built by the release workflow, and a source tree that runs on Linux with Python, Node and FFmpeg.
